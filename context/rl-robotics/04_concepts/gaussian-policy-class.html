Gaussian policy class

The policy family for a continuous command that adds isotropic Gaussian noise of scale σ to a differentiable mean μθ​(x), whose score pulls the action-space displacement u−μθ​(x) back through the Jacobian of the mean.

Where it sits

  • Unifying diagram cell: a policy × no dynamics model. It is the class that makes the policy row reachable for a torque command, where the value branch would need a maximisation over a continuous set at every control step.
  • Bellman object approximated: none. The class is the object being optimised, and no fixed-point equation is attached to it.
  • Failure mode addressed: none directly. Through σ it sets both the exploration of failure mode 4 and the estimator variance of failure mode 2, in opposite directions.
  • Course first appearance: Week 9

Core idea

A joint torque or a wheel speed is a point in Rm, and the deterministic controller a robotics engineer would write is a map x↦μ(x) into that space. The class keeps that map, parameterises it, and adds zero-mean noise so that the policy has a density to differentiate. The mean carries the control law and the noise carries the exploration, which is the same split an identification experiment makes between the nominal input and the probing signal injected on top of it.

Formal definition

With μθ​(x)∈Rm differentiable in θ and fixed isotropic covariance,
πθ​(u∣x)=N(μθ​(x),σ2I)=(2πσ2)−m/2exp(−2σ2∥u−μθ​(x)∥2​),
and writing ∇θ​μθ​(x)∈Rm×p for the Jacobian of the mean,
∇θ​logπθ​(u∣x)=σ21​(∇θ​μθ​(x))⊤(u−μθ​(x)).
The update therefore moves the mean towards the action that was sampled, by an amount set by how far that action fell and by how strongly each parameter moves the mean. For a scalar action with μθ​(x)=θ the Jacobian is 1 and the score is (u−θ)/σ2, with
E[∇θ​logπθ​(u∣x)]=0,Var(∇θ​logπθ​(u∣x))=σ4σ2​=σ21​.
Halving σ doubles the typical score magnitude and quadruples its variance. Structure enters through μθ​ and holds at every θ. A squashed mean μθ​(x)=umax​tanh(fθ​(x)) respects the actuator bound, parameters shared across a quadruped’s four legs impose a gait symmetry, and μθ​(x)=μctrl​(x)+Δθ​(x) learns a correction on top of a controller that already works.

Connection to classical control

This is a fixed-structure controller with a dither on the input. Iterative feedback tuning perturbs the parameters of such a controller, measures the closed-loop cost and steps down an estimated gradient, and extremum seeking does the same with a sinusoidal probe. The Gaussian class supplies the probe from the policy itself, and the Likelihood-ratio identity converts that randomness into an unbiased gradient rather than a finite difference. The persistent-excitation reasoning of identification transfers intact. A probe too small to move the output teaches nothing about the parameter, and here it also drives the score, so the same σ that stops exciting the plant stops informing the estimator.

Key limitation

The class is unimodal. If two well-separated actions are both good at a state and everything between them is bad, the mean can settle between the modes and command an action that is good at neither, which is exactly the situation a time-optimal bang-bang solution creates. A Softmax policy class over a torque grid represents both branches without difficulty, so the continuous class is not automatically the more expressive one. The support is also unbounded. Squashing the mean bounds what the policy aims at and not what it draws, so a broad policy still commands torques past umax​ and relies on the actuator to clip them. And exploration and estimation share one parameter, so a run in which σ collapses early has stopped exploring and has a badly conditioned estimator at the same time.
